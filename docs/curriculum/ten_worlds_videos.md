# Ten Worlds — the Watch → Talk → Do video layer (canonical)

Companion to [ten_worlds_prototype.jsx](ten_worlds_prototype.jsx). Added by the
user 2026-06-07. Each world carries 2–3 pre-screened short videos. **The video
is the spark, the activity is the fire.**

## The mechanic

**Watch (3 min) → Talk (1 min: "what did you notice?") → Do (the activity).**

The video is never the lesson. The video is the *invitation* to the lesson.
The doing is the learning. The screen goes dark, the hands come out. That's the
transition — every time.

> The rule: watch 3–5 minutes, then DO something because of what you watched.
> Never just watch. Always watch → do.

## Screen-time rules

- **One video per day maximum.** 3–5 minutes. Never longer than 7.
- The video always comes **BEFORE** an activity, never after. It's the spark,
  not the reward.
- **Lights off for nature and space videos** — make it an event.
- **Pause and ask "what did you notice?"** before doing the activity.
- **Never force a second viewing.** If they want to watch again, that's their
  choice during quiet time.

## Where to find them

Search YouTube for: "Pixar short films for kids," "BBC Earth clips for
children," "time-lapse nature for kids," "Sesame Street social emotional
learning," "StoryBots," "Mr. Rogers feelings." Make a **private playlist per
week** (3–5 pre-screened, ads skipped). Play from a laptop → screen/projector;
if no projector, gather around the laptop — **the huddle IS the experience.**

## The videos, by world

### Week 1 — World of Me
- **Sesame Street — "I Love My Hair"** (3 min): a girl sings about what makes
  her unique. → Each kid says one thing about their body they're proud of. Draw it.
- **Time-lapse of a baby learning to walk** (2 min): falling, standing, walking.
  → "That baby is YOU. What else couldn't you do that you can now?" Make the list.
- **Pixar — "Lou"** (6 min, no dialogue): a lost-and-found box comes alive.
  → "What's in YOUR lost and found? What have you lost? What have you found?" Draw both.

### Week 2 — World of Stories
- **"The Fantastic Flying Books of Mr. Morris Lessmore"** (15 min, show first 5):
  a man enters a world where books are alive. → "If your book could fly, where
  would it go?" Draw the journey.
- **A shadow puppet show** (3 min): stories told with hands and light.
  → Lights off, flashlight, make shadow stories on the wall. The room becomes a theater.
- **StoryBots — "Once Upon a Time"** (3 min): silly take on story structure.
  → Kids build their own story on the HOME → ROAD → DARK PLACE → NEW HOME map.

### Week 3 — World of Nature
- **BBC Earth — "Planet Earth" ants carrying food** (2 min, no narration).
  → Go outside, find ants, watch 5 min, draw what they're doing. Connect to CARRY.
- **Time-lapse of a seed sprouting** (2 min): a plant's whole life in 120s.
  → Check YOUR seed. Draw today's growth. Patience made visible.
- **"Octopus escaping through a tiny hole"** (1 min): no bones — it can squeeze
  through anything bigger than its beak. → Connect to SOLVE. What impossible
  space can YOU fit through? Try chair legs, under tables.

### Week 4 — World of Water
- **"Water cycle song for kids"** (3 min, animated). → Act out the water cycle
  with your body: curl (drop), spread (evaporate), float (cloud), fall (rain), flow (river).
- **Slow-motion of water drops hitting a surface** (2 min, no words).
  → Eyedropper on paper, foil, fabric, plastic. Each surface tells the water what to do.
- **"Blue Planet" coral reef** (3 min): lights off, project big — the room
  becomes underwater. → Draw what you saw, but draw it like you LIVE there. You're a fish.

### Week 5 — World of Music
- **"Stomp — kitchen scene"** (3 min): music from brooms, trash cans.
  → Grab anything in the room, make music from it. Everything is an instrument.
- **Bobby McFerrin — pentatonic scale with an audience** (3 min): a whole room
  sings without being taught. → Try it with the class. Your body already knows music.
- **"Kodo Japanese Drummers"** (2 min): massive drums, all body.
  → Drum circle with anything. Start slow, build speed. The room shakes.

### Week 6 — World of Space
- **"Pale Blue Dot" — Carl Sagan over the Voyager 1 photo** (3 min). Simplify:
  "This tiny dot is Earth. Everything you've ever known is on it." → Draw the dot. Draw YOU on it.
- **Time-lapse of the night sky / Milky Way** (2 min, no narration): all lights
  off, project big, silence. → Lie down, ceiling is the sky, glow stars named after feelings.
- **"How big is the universe" size comparison** (3 min): Earth → Sun → stars →
  galaxies. → The planet walk in the yard. Scale becomes physical.

### Week 7 — World of Dreams
- **"My Neighbor Totoro" — forest / cat bus scene** (3–5 min): dreamy, surreal,
  gentle. → "Draw a place that looks real but has one impossible thing in it."
- **"Inside Out" — the dream production scene** (3 min). → "YOU are the director.
  Tonight decide what you WANT to dream about. Tomorrow tell us if it worked."
- **Salvador Dalí's melting clocks** (still image, 60s of silent looking).
  → "In dreams, time doesn't work right." Draw a dream painting where one normal
  thing does something impossible.

### Week 8 — World of Time
- **Time-lapse of a flower blooming** (1 min): 3 days in 60s. → "What would YOUR
  week look like in 1 minute?" Draw your week as a comic, one panel per day.
- **"Evolution of humans in 5 minutes"** (animation). → Draw YOUR evolution:
  baby → toddler → now → teenager → adult → old. Six drawings, one life.
- **Any "how it's made" video** (3 min) — a pencil, crayon, ball. → "That pencil
  took 100 people, 4 materials, 2 days. You use it for 30 seconds." Pick any
  object: how long did it take to make? Who made it? The invisible time inside everything.

### Week 9 — World of Feelings
- **Pixar — "Partly Cloudy"** (5 min, no dialogue): frustration, loyalty, repair.
  → "The stork got hurt but kept coming back. Have you ever been hurt by a friend
  but stayed friends? Why?"
- **Mr. Rogers — "What do you do with the mad that you feel?"** (2 min). → Each
  kid answers: "What do YOU do with the mad that you feel?" Answers on the wall.
- **Different laughs from around the world** (2 min). → "Laughter sounds
  different everywhere but FEELS the same everywhere. What else feels the same
  everywhere?" List the universal feelings.

### Week 10 — World of Us
- **A slideshow YOU make** — photos from every week, set to music (3–5 min): the
  empty room, the first day, week by week, the room filling, the faces changing.
  Show the kids first, then the families at the gallery.
- **Pixar — "Bao"** (7 min): loving something and letting it go. → "What will you
  take with you? Not a thing — a FEELING or a SKILL or a WORD." Last sticky notes.
- **Watch their OWN work** — the story rivers, dream maps, body maps, photos.
  Walk the room like a museum. The video this week IS the room itself.

## How this maps onto the tooling

A video is a **pre-activity spark** attached to a world's activity — it slots
into the existing activity system, not a new one:

- Each `video` = `{title, minutes, after}` where `after` is the Talk-prompt +
  the activity it ignites. Stored per world in the curriculum catalog.
- The day-template builder can carry a "Watch" block kind (3–5 min) right before
  the activity block it sparks — the Watch → Do transition becomes a visible part
  of the day's shape.
- The screen-time rules are guidance surfaced on the world's "Today" card so a
  teacher (or sub) follows the discipline: one/day, before-not-after, lights off
  for nature/space, pause-and-ask.
